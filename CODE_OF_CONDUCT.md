# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text at that link is what applies; in short:

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are learning something new, in a language that may not be their first.
- Critique ideas, sources and code, never people.
- No harassment, insults, or sexualized language or imagery, and no publishing others' private information.
- Assume good faith, and accept correction gracefully (this course depends on it).

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer, [@Jims2333](https://github.com/Jims2333),
privately through the contact options on their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled confidentially.
The maintainer may remove comments, issues or contributions that violate this code, and may block repeat offenders.
